A bluedoc review puts each finding under the lines it is about, in a diff the server reads from git, with the agent's recommendation starred. For 1.1 I went through it with one question in mind: what still needs me?

## A card per PR

Each PR is a card. Its header has the number with a fold chevron, the title, an answered meter that fills as you go, and a status: N replies when the agent's replies wait for you, Answered once nothing is open. On the right sit the branches, head → base, with a chip per severity.

As you scroll the findings, the title row and toolbar stick under the app bar; the rest of the header scrolls away.

## One toolbar

The toolbar under each PR's title holds it all: All · Open · Done with counts, Accept recommended, Expand all, Copy and Reset under ⋯, and Checklist · Diff · Merged. Every action works in every view, a pick in one view shows in the others, and each PR keeps its own filter and view.

A review opens on Open: findings with no answer, a Tell me more not sent yet, or an agent reply you haven't answered. A finding you answer stays until you move on; the list ends with "N findings you answered are hidden · Show". Findings that need you load open; answered ones fold to ✓ and your pick.

The tracker in the Contents pane counts what is open. Click it and it opens the first finding that needs you, scrolls to it and flashes it.

## The code, in the finding

Every finding opens whole, with More details under its title: the detail, then the file and the lines around the comment, and Open in Diff view to jump there.

To comment on a line rather than a finding, turn on Element (C) and click the line. The comment names the diff, the file and the line, so the agent knows exactly where.

## The send key says what happens

Who owns the PR decides what your answers become, and the send key names it:

- **Reply and fix** on your own PR. The agent gathers your peers' open threads and checks each against the code; you pick Fix in PR, Ticket or Decline. It fixes on your branch, replies on each thread and resolves the fixed ones.
- **Post review** on someone else's PR. You can't change their code, so each finding is a comment to Post or Don't post, and Post as sends each PR's review as a Comment, Request changes or Approve.
- **Apply decisions** on your own change, before there is a PR.

Comments on findings and lines go with Request changes. The send dialog lists every pick, and under one that differs from the agent's, what it recommended.

<Video clip="review-send" caption="Accept recommended takes the starred answers, one is changed to Ticket, and Reply and fix lists all 11 picks." />

When the agent answers in a finding's conversation, its reply is signed with its model's name and marked New until you've seen it.
